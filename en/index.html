02 — Work

In practice

Mobile app · Android · 2026

Underground Fight Club

A boxing and kickboxing club in Pirot, Serbia, ran its sessions through a WhatsApp group and tracked attendance and fees in a paper notebook. We built an app that does both jobs faster.

  • One-tap RSVPMembers see the week's sessions on the home screen and confirm with a single tap, faster than liking a message in the group.
  • A roster instead of a notebookThe trainer sees who said they're coming and only corrects the exceptions. After the session, attendance records itself.
  • A balance instead of tick marksEvery member has one balance: payments minus sessions. The manager types an amount and the app works out what's paid.

Built with: Expo · React Native · Supabase · PostgreSQL
